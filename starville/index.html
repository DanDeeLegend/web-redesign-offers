About our school

Our children come first.

Welcome to Starville School, one of Nigeria's premier educational institutions, dedicated to child development and enriching extracurricular activities.

We have thoughtfully created a learning community focused on the holistic growth of each child. At Starville, children are treated as family and brought up with the utmost care possible.

Our vision
To provide a well-rounded education that equips all learners with lifelong skills to face the challenges and demands of the 21st century and beyond.
Our mission
To provide all learners with outstanding learning experiences through quality teaching and world-class facilities, where every child feels safe, happy and motivated to excel.
