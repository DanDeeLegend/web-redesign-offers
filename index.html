Welcome to LBIS

The best of British education, rooted in Nigeria.

Since 2007, Lead British International School has combined the English National Curriculum with Nigerian standards and values, giving children a world-class education without losing sight of home.

In small classes, our teachers know every child well. They stretch able learners, support those who need it, and build the confidence, character and curiosity that last well beyond school.

  • Academic excellence

    Rigorous British teaching with clear routes to IGCSE, WAEC and university.

  • Character & care

    A safe, nurturing community where responsibility and respect come first.

  • Global outlook

    Strong in STEM, the creative arts and languages, ready for a global future.
